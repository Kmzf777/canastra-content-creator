── WHAT YOU NEED FIRST ──
API credentials Amazon has already approved — a client ID, a client secret and a
refresh token, for whichever API you want. Three values.

The browser "Add custom connector" route does NOT work. Amazon's Ads server asks for a
permission its own login server does not hand out to unapproved apps, so you get a 400
error before you reach your account. The setup here goes around that.
