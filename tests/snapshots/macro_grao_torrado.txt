Extreme macro photograph of one single roasted Arara coffee bean lying on a
brushed stainless steel cupping tray with faint circular use marks. Roast level: medium-dark, taken just past first crack.
The centre cut runs the length of the bean, silverskin still lodged inside the crease,
dry and pale against the darker shell. Coffee oils bead unevenly along the ridges and
throw small specular highlights; the rest of the surface stays matte, porous, faintly
cratered where the cell walls collapsed during the roast.
Lighting: hard raking light from camera left, one diffused source only, no fill card, deep falloff into
the shadow side so the relief of the seam reads clearly.
100mm macro lens at f/5.6, focus on the centre cut, background falling off into unlit
dark grey. Shot handheld on a workbench, not on a light table.
Loose chaff flakes and a light dusting of ground coffee sit off to one side where they
happened to fall, not arranged.
Palette: burnt umber, warm sienna, cool steel grey. Natural contrast, true blacks,
no glow, no bloom.
